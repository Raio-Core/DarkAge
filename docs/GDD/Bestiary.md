# 📖 Bestiary: Creatures of the Divided Realm

> *"Know thy enemy. In the Divided Realm, that knowledge could save your soul—or damn you eternally."*
> — **Covenant Field Manual**

---

## Overview

The Divided Realm is home to countless creatures, from mundane animals to terrifying Void entities. This bestiary documents the known threats that adventurers may encounter.

---

## Combat Tips

1. **Scout First**: Identify enemy types before engaging
2. **Know Your Enemy**: Different enemies require different tactics
3. **Bring the Right Damage**: Fire for trolls, magic for golems
4. **Focus Fire**: Kill high-threat targets first
5. **Watch for Reinforcements**: Many enemies call for help
