# 📜 History & Lore: The Age of Division

> *"History is written by the victors, but in the Divided Realm, even the victors cannot agree on what happened. Every Echo remembers differently."*
> — **Seraphina the Many**

---

## The Age Before: The Unified World

### The First Era (Year -10,000)

Before the Veil, there was one world. It was called **Aetheria**—the realm of endless possibility. Magic flowed freely, and the boundaries between what was possible and impossible were thin.

**Key Events**:
- **Year -10,000**: First humans discover Soul Shards
- **Year -9,500**: The First Keepers emerge—powerful wizards who understand the true nature of reality
- **Year -9,000**: Magic becomes institutionalized; the first Covenants form
- **Year -8,500**: The Great Library of All Knowledge built at the world's center

### The Age of Discovery (Year -8,000 to -5,000)

The Covenants, originally scholarly organizations, grew in power and influence. They discovered the nature of the Void between realities and began experimenting with Soul Shard manipulation.

**Key Events**:
- **Year -8,000**: Discovery of the Void—empty space between realities
- **Year -7,500**: First attempts at crossing between Echoes
- **Year -7,000**: The First Keepers create the Veil—the barrier between the Ordered Realm and the Wild Echoes
- **Year -6,500**: The Great Betrayal—the First Keepers split into factions
- **Year -5,500**: The Veil becomes permanent; the Divided Realm is established

---

## The Age of Order: The Covenant's Rise

### The Covenant's Dominion (Year -5,000 to -1,000)

After the Veil was established, the Covenants took control of the Ordered Realm. They declared themselves protectors of humanity, claiming the Veil was necessary to protect people from the dangerous magic of the Wild Echoes.

**Key Events**:
- **Year -5,000**: The Twelve High Covenant formed
- **Year -4,500**: The Confessors are created—women trained to touch and manipulate Soul Shards
- **Year -4,000**: The Fractured Citadel is built—seat of Covenant power
- **Year -3,500**: All non-Covenant magic is declared illegal
- **Year -2,500**: The Ordered Realm reaches its peak of control
- **Year -1,000**: First signs of unrest

### The Great Exodus (Year -1,000 to -500)

Unable to bear the Covenant's control, thousands flee to the Wild Echoes. These refugees become the first Unbound.

**Key Events**:
- **Year -1,000**: The First Shardwalkers escape to the Wild Echoes
- **Year -800**: Westbrook is founded as the last settlement before the Veil
- **Year -600**: The Unbound establish their first permanent settlements in the Echoes
- **Year -500**: The Covenants attempt to close the Veil but fail

---

## The Age of Conflict: War and Resistance

### The Shardwalker Wars (Year -500 to 0)

Open conflict between the Covenants and the Unbound.

**Key Events**:
- **Year -500**: The First War of the Veil begins
- **Year -400**: Kael's ancestor leads the Deserters—Covenant soldiers who refuse to kill refugees
- **Year -300**: The Bone Woman (Lyra's predecessor) discovers how to heal damaged Soul Shards
- **Year -200**: The Unweaver is born
- **Year -100**: The Unweaver begins gathering followers
- **Year 0**: The Second Great Betrayal—the Unweaver's true plan revealed

### The Present Day (Year 0 to Present)

The game begins here. The world is in turmoil as the Unweaver's influence grows.

**Timeline of Events**:
- **Year 0**: Player's awakening—first manifestation of Shard Sight
- **Year 0-1**: The player crosses the Veil for the first time
- **Year 1+**: The war between Covenants, Unbound, and the Unweaver escalates

## Faction Histories

### The Covenants

*"Order is protection. Freedom is chaos. We maintain the Veil for your safety."*

**Origin**: The original scholarly organizations that discovered the nature of Soul Shards

**Philosophy**: 
- Magic must be controlled to prevent destruction
- The Veil protects humanity from dangerous powers
- The individual must be subordinate to the collective

**Philosophy**:

- Every individual has the right to choose their own path
- The Covenants are oppressors who must be overthrown

**Origin**: Unknown—appeared in the Echoes around Year -200

**Philosophy**:
- Freedom leads to suffering

## Important Locations

### Westbrook

**Founded**: Year -800
**Population**: 50,000
**Government**: Council of elders (diminished from former glory)

Westbrook was once a thriving waypoint, the last stop before the Veil. Now it's a place of quiet desperation, where those who wish to flee the Covenants gather in secret.

### The Fractured Citadel

**Built**: Year -4,000
**Purpose**: Seat of Covenant power

A fortress-city of white stone and crystal spires, the Citadel is beautiful in a cold way. Everything is monitored, everything is controlled. It's the heart of the Ordered Realm.

### The Unwoven Realm

**Established**: Year -100
**Population**: Unknown (appears normal but citizens have no will)

The Unweaver's echo—a world where "peace" has been achieved through the complete elimination of free will. Everyone smiles. Everyone is happy. Nothing is real.

## The Void Entities

### The Night Whispers

> *"They speak in voices you want to hear, offering truths you need to hear, leading to destinations you should never reach."*

The most dangerous Void creatures—they offer knowledge and power in exchange for your sanity.

### The Hunger

The Void itself. Not a creature but a place—the absence of everything. Those who fall into the Hunger are consumed entirely.

### The Echoed

> *"I remember being human. I remember having a name. Now I am fragments of a person scattered across realities."*

Those who became trapped between Echoes, their identities fractured across multiple realities.

## Magic History

### Discovery of Soul Shards (Year -10,000)

The first humans discover that every living being has a crystalline core—a Soul Shard—that connects body to spirit.

### Development of Additive Magic (Year -9,000 to -7,000)

The Covenants develop methods to manipulate Soul Shards in positive ways—healing, protecting, binding.

### Discovery of Subtractive Magic (Year -8,000)

The forbidden arts are discovered—methods to destroy and consume Soul Shards.

### The Confessor's Creation (Year -4,500)

A technique is developed to Add the Confessor's will while Subtracting the victim's identity—the horror of the Hollow.

### The Modern Day

Magic is rare, regulated, and feared. The player is among the few who can See—and must choose how to use this power.
