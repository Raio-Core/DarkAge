# Expanded Factions of the Divided Realm

> *"In the fractured world, allegiance is everything. Choose wisely—your faction determines not just your allies, but your very destiny."*

The Divided Realm is teeming with factions beyond the three major powers. This comprehensive guide details every faction, sub-faction, and organization that players can encounter, join, or oppose.

---

## Major Factions

### The Covenant

The dominant force of order, the Covenant believes that safety comes through control. Their golden banners fly over fortified cities, and their iron fist maintains peace—through force if necessary.

#### Leadership Structure

| Rank | Title | Role |
|------|-------|------|
| 1 | **The Eternal Sovereign** | Supreme ruler, believed immortal |
| 2 | **Archon Council** | 7 advisors governing major decisions |
| 3 | **High Wardens** | Regional military commanders |
| 4 | **Wardens** | City and territory governors |
| 5 | **Knights** | Elite soldiers and officers |
| 6 | **Justiciars** | Law enforcers and investigators |
| 7 | **Citizens** | Common population |

#### Covenant Sub-Factions

**The Order of the Golden Flame**
- Religious arm of the Covenant
- Priests who wield both faith and flame
- Quest givers for holy missions
- Players can become Initiates → Acolytes → Priests → High Priests

**The Iron Wardens**
- Military police force
- Maintain order in cities
- Accept bounty hunting contracts
- Reputation required for advanced bounties

**The Archive Keepers**
- Scholars and historians
- Maintain knowledge of the world
- Offer research quests
- Provide lore and backstory

**The Healing Light**
- Medical corps
- Heal Covenant soldiers and citizens
- Can be joined by any player
- Provides healing buffs in territory

**The Shadow Inquisitors**
- Secret police
- Root out corruption and heresy
- Dangerous missions for high rewards
- Requires high Covenant reputation

---

### The Unbound

The Unbound are those who rejected the Covenant's control, finding freedom in the wilds and chaos of the world. They are fragmented but passionate, dangerous but independent.

#### Leadership Structure

| Rank | Title | Role |
|------|-------|------|
| 1 | **The Free Council** | Ruling body of clan leaders |
| 2 | **Clan Chieftains** | Leaders of major clans |
| 3 | **Warleaders** | Military commanders |
| 4 | **Elders** | Wise counselors |
| 5 | **Free Warriors** | Standard fighters |
| 6 | **Initiates** | New members |

#### Unbound Sub-Factions

**The Wildspeaker Tribe**
- Druids and nature magic users
- Speak for the land itself
- Quest givers for nature-related missions
- Can learn beast speech and nature magic

**The Pirate Lords**
- Sea-based Unbound
- Control maritime trade routes
- Smuggling and trading opportunities
- Requires sailing and combat skills

**The Ronin Brotherhood**
- Masterless samurai
- Hire themselves as mercenaries
- High-damage combat quests
- Exclusive weapon techniques

**The Sky Raiders**
- Airship pirates
- Attack Covenant supply lines
- Flight mount requirements
- Unique aerial combat

**The Beast Tamers**
- Tame wild creatures
- Provide mount and companion services
- Creature capture quests
- Exclusive taming abilities

---

### The Unweaver's Cult

The Unweaver preaches surrender—that true peace comes from giving oneself to the Void. Their followers are numerous, devoted, and terrifying in their conviction.

#### Leadership Structure

| Rank | Title | Role |
|------|-------|------|
| 1 | **The Unweaver** | The Prophet, voice of the Void |
| 2 | **Void Speakers** | Direct communicators with the Void |
| 3 | **High Cultists** | Regional leaders |
| 4 | **Cultists** | Devoted followers |
| 5 | **Initiates** | New converts |

#### Unweaver Sub-Factions

**The Whispering Void**
- Elite Void magic users
- Most powerful spellcasters
- Dark ritual quests
- Unique Void abilities

**The Quiet Ones**
- Assassins and spies
- Silent elimination specialists
- Stealth and poison missions
- Reputation-gated contracts

**The Hollowed Ones**
- Those who gave themselves to the Void
- Become void-touched beings
- Transformation quests
- Permanent stat changes

**The Dream Walkers**
- Masters of sleep and illusion
- Manipulate dreams and minds
- Psychological manipulation quests
- Unique debuff abilities

## Player-Created Factions

### Guilds

Player guilds can rise to become recognized factions through achievements:

**Faction Levels**
- **Guild** - Basic player group (up to 500 members)
- **House** - Recognized organization (requires territory control)
- **Kingdom** - Major faction status (requires 3+ territories)
- **Empire** - Server-dominating power (requires majority control)

**Guild Ranks**
- Guild Master
- Officers (10 max)
- Veterans
- Members
- Initiates

**Guild Features**
- Guild bank with permissions
- Guild hall (customizable)
- Guild chat and voice
- Shared quests
- Territory control

## Faction Relationships

### Dynamic Reputation System

Every faction has relationship levels:

| Level | Name | Effects |
|-------|------|---------|
| -1000 | Nemesis | Attack on sight |
| -500 | Hostile | Won't trade, attacks if provoked |
| -100 | Unfriendly | Higher prices, limited services |
| 0 | Neutral | Standard interactions |
| 100 | Friendly | Small discounts, basic quests |
| 500 | Honored | Good discounts, exclusive quests |
| 1000 | Revered | Major discounts, unique items |
| 2500 | Exalted | Legendary status, faction champion |

## Faction Wars

### Territorial Control

Factions fight for control of regions:

**Control Benefits**
- Tax revenue
- Resource access
- Quest availability
- NPC presence
- Defense bonuses

**Control Levels**
- **Contested:** PvP enabled, unstable
- **Faction Control:** 51%+ influence
- **Dominant:** 75%+ influence
- **Total:** 90%+ influence

### The Great War

Major server events where factions clash:

**Phases**
1. Skirmishes (small battles)
2. Battles (medium fights)
3. Sieges (territory capture)
4. Final Assault (server-wide event)

**Rewards**
- Exclusive weapons
- Territory control
- Faction reputation
- Unique titles

## Cross-Faction Mechanics

### Renegades

Players can leave their faction but face consequences:

**Leaving Penalties**
- Reputation reset with old faction
- Can't return for 30 days
- Bounty placed by old faction
- Some content locked

### Diplomatic Players

Special status for those who maintain multiple faction relationships:

**Requirements**
- 500+ reputation with 2+ opposing factions
- Neutral with major factions
- Complete diplomatic quests

**Benefits**
- Trade with all factions
- Peaceful passage through most territories
- Unique cross-faction quests
