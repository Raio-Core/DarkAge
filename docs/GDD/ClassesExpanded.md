# Class Mastery: Complete Class Guide

> *"Your class is your destiny, but your choices define your legacy." — The Class Master's Wisdom*

The Divided Realm offers deep class customization with base classes, specializations, and advanced masteries. This guide details every class option available to players.

---

## Base Classes

### Warrior

The master of arms and armor. Warriors are the backbone of any party, protecting allies and crushing enemies.

#### Core Stats
- **Primary:** Strength
- **Secondary:** Constitution
- **Weapon Types:** All melee weapons, shields

#### Class Features

| Level | Feature | Description |
|-------|---------|-------------|
| 1 | Battlecry | AoE buff/debuff |
| 5 | Shield Block | Active block ability |
| 10 | Whirlwind | Spin attack |
| 15 | Berserker Rage | Damage boost |
| 20 | Battle Master | Combat techniques |
| 30 | Weapon Mastery | Ultimate weapon skill |
| 40 | Avatar of War | Transform into war machine |

#### Warrior Specializations

**1. Paladin (Holy Warrior)**
- **Role:** Tank/Support
- **Key Ability:** Holy magic + shield
- **Aura of Protection:** Grant shields to allies
- **Holy Strike:** Smite enemies
- **Divine Shield:** Ultimate defense
- **Playstyle:** Defensive, supportive

**2. Berserker (Madness)**
- **Role:** Damage Dealer
- **Key Ability:** Rage mechanics
- **Blood Fury:** Damage boost when low health
- **Cleave:** Multi-target attacks
- **Death Wish:** Ultimate damage
- **Playstyle:** Aggressive, risky

**3. Guardian (Protector)**
- **Role:** Tank
- **Key Ability:** Taunt, shield wall
- **Taunt:** Force enemies to attack
- **Shield Wall:** Massive defense
- **Last Stand:** Survival ultimate
- **Playstyle:** Defensive, party protection

**4. Warlord (Commander)**
- **Role:** Support/Buffer
- **Key Ability:** buffs
- **Rally:** Boost ally stats
- **Commander's Voice:** Group buff
- **Lead the Charge:** Ultimate buff
- **Playstyle:** Supportive, strategic

## Cross-Class Combinations

### Hybrid Classes

Players can mix two specializations:

| Combination | Result |
|-------------|--------|
| Warrior + Mage | Spellblade |
| Rogue + Priest | Shadow Priest |
| Ranger + Druid | Beast Druid |
| Mage + Necromancer | Dark Mage |
| Monk + Bard | Performance Fighter |

### Prestige Classes

Advanced classes for max-level players:

**1. Death Knight**
- Requirements: Warrior 40, Necromancer 20
- Undead warrior with dark powers

**2. Arcane Paladin**
- Requirements: Paladin 30, Elementalist 10
- Holy + elemental magic

**3. Shadow Assassin**
- Requirements: Assassin 30, Shadowblade 10
- Ultimate stealth killer

### Leveling Path

| Level Range | Focus |

| 20-30 | Master specialization |

### Skill Trees

Each specialization has three trees:
- **Primary Tree:** Core abilities
- **Secondary Tree:** Support abilities
- **Utility Tree:** Passive buffs
