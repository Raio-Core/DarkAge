# The Mystic Arts: Complete Magic System

> *"Magic is not power—it is understanding. To master the arcane is to master reality itself." — Archmage Valdris*

The Divided Realm contains dozens of magical schools, each with unique spells, philosophies, and practitioners. This comprehensive guide details every magic system available to players.

---

## The Two Primordial Forces

All magic derives from two fundamental energies:

### Additive Magic (Aether)
Magic that adds, creates, enhances, and protects.
- **Manifestations:** Light, healing, creation, enhancement, protection
- **Source:** The Aetherial Plane
- **Users:** Priests, paladins, white mages, nature magic users
- **Color:** Gold, white, silver, pale blue

### Subtractive Magic (Void)
Magic that removes, destroys, corrupts, and transforms.
- **Manifestations:** Darkness, death, destruction, corruption, entropy
- **Source:** The Void Between
- **Users:** Necromancers, dark mages, shadow users
- **Color:** Black, deep purple, crimson, sickly green

### Neutral Magic (Essence)
Magic that manipulates, transforms, and connects.
- **Manifestations:** Elemental, enchanting, transmutation, divination
- **Source:** The Material Plane itself
- **Users:** Wizards, sorcerers, druids, elementalists
- **Color:** Rainbow, pure white, deep blue

- One-time use spells
- Can be cast without knowing magic
- Rare and valuable

- Channel magic through
- Increase spell power

## Magic Progression

### Learning Spells

**Methods**
1. **Teachers:** Find NPCs who teach spells
2. **Scrolls:** Learn from scroll practice
3. **Research:** Discover through experimentation
4. **Spirit:** Learn from spirits and ancestors
5. **Memory:** Unlock through Memory System

### Spell Mastery

**Mastery Levels**
- **Novice:** Basic casting
- **Adept:** 10% bonus damage
- **Expert:** 25% bonus, new effects
- **Master:** 50% bonus, ultimate form
- **Grandmaster:** 100% bonus, instant cast

### Spell Modifications

**Through Equipment**
- **Focus Items:** Reduce casting time
- **Casting Rods:** Reduce mana cost
- **Spell Amplifiers:** Increase damage
- **Mana Regeneration:** Cast more spells

**Through Talents**
- **Dual Casting:** Cast two spells simultaneously
- **Chain Casting:** Next spell is free
- **Empowered:** 50% more powerful
- **Quickened:** Instant cast

## Magic in Combat

### Casting Types

| Type | Description | Counter |
|------|-------------|---------|
| **Channeled** | Must stay still | Interrupt |
| **Instant** | Instant effect | None |
| **Ground** | Place on ground | Dodge |
| **Projectile** | Travel to target | Block/Evade |
| **Summon** | Call creature | Kill before arrival |

### Magic vs. Physical

| Defense Type | Blocks |
|--------------|--------|
| Magic Resistance | Reduces magic damage |
| Spell Reflection | Returns to caster |
| Magic Immunity | No damage |
| Dispel | Removes buffs |
