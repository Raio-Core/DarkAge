# The World of Dark Age: Complete Atlas

> *"To know the land is to master it. Every region holds secrets, treasures, and dangers." — The Cartographer's Guide*

The Divided Realm is a vast world of diverse regions, each with unique landscapes, creatures, resources, and mysteries. This comprehensive guide details every explorable area.

---

## The Known World Map

The world is divided into major regions, each controlled by different factions and containing unique environments.

---

## The Covenant Territories

### 1. Solara Prime

**Description:** The capital region of the Covenant, featuring the golden city of Solara and surrounding fertile lands.

**Terrain:** Rolling hills, golden wheat fields, ancient forests
**Climate:** Mild, sunny most of the year
**Faction Control:** Covenant (Strong)

**Key Locations**

| Location | Type | Level Range | Notable Features |
|----------|------|-------------|-------------------|
| Solara | Capital City | 1-80 | Throne, temples, merchants |
| Golden Fields | Farmland | 1-20 | Crops, peaceful |
| Sunfire Keep | Fortress | 30-50 | Training grounds |
| Dawn Academy | Magic School | 40-70 | Arcane training |
| The Sun Spire | Dungeon | 60-80 | Final boss: Solar Dragon |

**NPCs**
- Queen Elara III - Ruler
- Lord Commander Voss - Military
- Archmage Valdris - Magic

**Quest Hubs**
- Sunfire Temple (Main Story)
- Market District (Crafting)
- Barracks (Combat)
