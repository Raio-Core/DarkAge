# The Creatures of the Divided Realm: Complete Bestiary

> *"Know thy enemy as thyself. In the Divided Realm, this is survival." — Monster Hunter's Codex*

This comprehensive guide catalogs every creature, beast, and monstrosity that roams the world. From the smallest insect to the ancient dragons, every creature has a place in the ecosystem of terror.

---

## Tier 1: Minor Creatures

### Vermin

Small creatures that swarm and infest. Generally harmless alone but dangerous in numbers.

#### Rabid Rats
- **Habitat:** Sewers, caves, ruins
- **Behavior:** Pack hunters, flee from fire
- **Weakness:** Fire, light
- **Drops:** Rat tails, disease samples

#### Cave Spiders
- **Habitat:** Caves, dark forests
- **Behavior:** Web builders, ambush predators
- **Weakness:** Fire, slashing
- **Drops:** Spider silk, venom

#### Giant Beetles
- **Habitat:** Underground, ruins
- **Behavior:** Scavengers, calm unless threatened
- **Weakness:** Fire, bludgeoning
- **Drops:** Beetle shells, chitin

#### Swarms of Bats
- **Habitat:** Caves, ruins, forests
- **Behavior:** Nocturnal, echolocation
- **Weakness:** Bright light, sonic attacks
- **Drops:** Bat wings, echolocation sense

#### Locust Hordes
- **Habitat:** Plains, farmlands
- **Behavior:** Migratory, destroy crops
- **Weakness:** Fire, cold
- **Drops:** Bug parts, chitin

## Creature Spawn Mechanics

### Spawn Types

| Type | Description | Respawn Time |
|------|-------------|--------------|
| Static | Always in same location | Instant |
| Patrol | Follows path | 10 minutes |
| Random | Random spawn | 5 minutes |
| Timed | Specific times | Varies |
| Event | World events | Event duration |
| Boss | Weekly/rare | Weekly |
| World Boss | Server events | Monthly |

### Spawn Conditions

- **Time-based:** Day/night, seasons
- **Weather-based:** Rain, storm, snow
- **Player-based:** Player triggers
- **World-based:** Server events
- **Location-based:** Entering area

## Creature Difficulty Scaling

### Level Scaling
- **Regional:** Scales to player level
- **Personal:** Individual creature scaling
- **Elite:** +5 levels
- **Rare:** +10 levels, unique
- **Boss:** +15+ levels
- **World Boss:** Scales to server

| Mode | Health | Damage | Drops |

| Normal | 1x | 1x | 1x |
